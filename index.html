Who We Are

We've Seen What Burnout Does

Restore By The Shore is the vision of Kate Poole, supported by her husband Gavin. They are a Christian couple living above a modest garden flat in Penzance, Cornwall. Having watched dear friends, vicars, youth workers, clergy, and charity staff give everything they have to the work of Jesus and quietly fall apart, they wanted to offer a place of rest and renewal to those serving the Christian faith.

Kate loves people and values getting to know the stories behind the work they do. She cares deeply about helping those who spend their lives caring for everyone else. Sometimes the church asks a great deal of the people serving most faithfully, and those same people do not always receive the rest or support they need themselves. Opening this space feels like one small way to share the blessing she has been given.

This Place Is for You If...

You are employed by, or volunteering with, a Christian organisation or registered charity (evidence required), and you are facing or recovering from burnout.

Our Faith and Our Mission

Our hope is to offer a safe, welcoming and peaceful space where those who have given so much in service to others can come and simply be for a while. We also recognise that cost can be a barrier to taking time away, so we aim to keep each stay affordable.

A Real Home

The flat is self-catered, clean, comfortable and well cared for, but it is also a real lived-in home. We are in a quiet residential area around a seven-minute walk from Penzance town centre, and we kindly ask guests to respect our neighbours and the local community.

Limited Accessibility

The lower-level garden flat is all on one level once inside, with just a small step into the living area. However, access is via a flight of steep and narrow old stone steps.

Unfortunately, this means we are currently unable to accommodate guests with mobility difficulties, and we are sorry for this limitation.

Getting to Us

There is no on-site parking, but we are close to Penzance train station, and travelling by train is often the easiest way to reach us. We warmly encourage guests to arrive by public transport where possible, and we are happy to offer a complimentary pick-up from the train or bus station by prior arrangement.

Lower Level Garden Flat

Self-Catered and Simply Peaceful

This two-bedroom, self-catered garden flat offers a peaceful place for rest and reflection. Occupying the lower level of our home, the space is entirely your own and you will have full privacy. We are here if you need anything, including company or prayer, but we will not intrude on your rest.

We make sure the basics are there waiting for you on arrival. All other food is self-catered, and there are local shops and restaurants nearby.

There is no television here, and we think that can be a gift. Bring your own devices if needed, and enjoy books and games during your stay.

Partners and up to two children are welcome. The flat has one double bedroom and one twin room, making it suitable for a family of four.

A well-behaved dog is welcome, but please let us know in advance.

There is a lovely enclosed garden at the front of the property for you to sit in and enjoy.

The Last Stop Before the Sea

Penzance is the most south-westerly town in mainland Britain. Step outside and the Atlantic is right there. We find the Cornish coast has a way of restoring perspective, and our hope is that it might do the same for you.

Things to Do

Our Favourite Attractions

A long and beautiful seafront walk beside Jubilee Pool, the UK's largest seawater lido, as well as its geothermal pool.

A picturesque seaside town looking across Mount's Bay to St Michael's Mount, the iconic tidal island with its medieval castle.

Just ten miles away is the end of mainland Britain. Nearby, the Minack Theatre offers an unforgettable open-air setting carved into the cliffs above Porthcurno.

A sheltered National Trust garden just outside Penzance, known for subtropical planting and peaceful woodland walks.

We are blessed with fresh fish, proper Cornish pasties, and Newlyn Art Gallery, which are all shaped by the extraordinary Atlantic light.

The South West Coast Path runs through Penzance and offers beautiful cliff walks at whatever pace feels right.
